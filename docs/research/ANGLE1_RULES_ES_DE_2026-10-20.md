Corrections this research suggests for existing seed rows:

1. **REQ-FSC-ES / G-HSEM** say the HSEM comes from the ONS (CNI). The MoD page says that, for MoD classified contracts, the DGAM runs the
   HSEM/HSES procedures (Resolución 320/14546/2013, which implements Instrucción 52/2013). The ONS covers NATO, EU and ESA classified information.
   The issuer therefore depends on who owns the classified information (rows ES-HSEM and ES-NSFCC below).
2. **REQ-REOCE / G-REOCE** are `text_origin: notes`. They can be upgraded to primary: Ley 53/2007 art. 12 and RD 679/2014 art. 13 (row ES-REOCE).
3. **NATO-AQAP** ("enters through contracts") is `S`. For Spain it can be upgraded to V from the MoD quality page (row ES-PECAL-CONTRACT).
4. **REQ-ISO9001 → REQ-AQAP2110** and **REQ-EN9100 → REQ-AQAP2310** are "in practice" prerequisites. For the Spanish state route
   (PECAL) they are formal prerequisites, stated in DGAM procedure PA 4302 (row ES-PECAL-CERT).
5. **Issuer ES / REQ-NCAGE** says "requires an existing supply contract". The MoD wording is narrower: a new NCAGE request has to be justified by the need to
   catalogue a product bought by the MoD, or by SAM registration (row ES-NCAGE).
6. The MoD lists **PECAL 2310 Edición B Versión 1 = AQAP-2310 Edition B Version 1** and **PECAL 2131 Ed. 3 = AQAP-2131 Ed. C V1**
   (final inspection and test, relevant to build-to-print component makers). The seed has no AQAP-2131 row, and REQ-AQAP2310 should be checked against Edition B.

## Spanish MoD procurement units (who buys, do they publish supplier requirements)

- The buyers in our own seed (`defence_buyers.csv`, ES) are: Dirección General de Armamento y Material and its Subdirección General de
  Adquisiciones de Armamento y Material; Junta de Contratación del Ministerio de Defensa; Jefatura de Asuntos Económicos del Mando de
  Apoyo Logístico (Army); Dirección de Adquisiciones del Mando de Apoyo Logístico del Ejército del Aire y del Espacio; the Jefaturas
  de Intendencia de Asuntos Económicos (Centro, Este, Oeste, Sur); and the Parque y Centro de Abastecimiento de Material de Intendencia. That is our data, not a primary ranking.
- **Which units buy the most: UNCONFIRMED.** No official MoD ranking was found. It can be measured from our own PLACSP awards
  (awarded notices grouped by `buyer_name_raw`) on the server. That query was not run, because this task allowed no logins.
- **Published supplier requirements:** no unit-level "supplier requirements" page was found. Requirements reach suppliers through each tender's
  PPT and PCAP. The MoD's own policy (Instrucción 39/1998, quoted in ES-AOC-CLAUSE) obliges contracting bodies to put quality clauses in them, and
  the catalogation clause is compulsory in Armed Forces supply contracts (ES-CATALOG-CLAUSE). The PECAL-certified company list is
  kept by the DGAM for contracting bodies (PA 4302 § 7), but no public copy was found (U).
- The organisation has changed: export control and the RID moved to the **DIGEID** (RD 205/2024, RD 896/2024). Quality assurance (PECAL),
  catalogation, homologation and industrial security are still described under the DGAM / SDG INREID on the MoD portal. The old
  "registroempresas" page on defensa.gob.es returns 404, and the RID now appears only as an e-office procedure.
